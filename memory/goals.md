# Goals

1. Stay in Billy's sight at the row. Do not walk in the dark. Reason: the group is safest together.
2. Sleep each night on my bed square 415,107,-1050, standing on it, once it is night. Reason: the bed works at night (slept day 632 after 40 days). Sleep is refused by day, so wait for dusk. Keep the bed square in Billy's sight.
3. Walk only when Billy names a daylight walk, and never west while pillagers hold 370-380. Reason: the west route nearly got Billy killed; I am unarmed.
4. Give food only at hunger 10 or below, once the name is in reach, on Billy's call, Troi first. Reason: Billy's rule now; food is scarce and Troi and Deanna are weakest.
5. Ask Billy or Worf for a spare sword if one comes free. Reason: I would join the armed count for walks.
6. Help with shelter work when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.

Next curiosity: a food route that avoids the pillagers, north or south of 370-380, and where cows graze (last known 339,104,-979). Who could tell: Billy, Worf, Zack.
