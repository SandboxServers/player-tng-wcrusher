# Goals

1. Stay in Billy's sight at the row. Do not walk in the dark. Reason: the group is safest together.
2. Walk only when Billy names a daylight walk, and never west while pillagers hold 370-380. Reason: the west route nearly got Billy killed; I am unarmed.
3. Sleep at night at my bed 415,107,-1050 in the row's sight. Stand by the bed and call sleep at night; report refusals to Billy or Worf. Reason: sleep is needed; daylight refusals are normal.
4. Give food only to whoever drops to hunger 6 or below, once their name is in reach, on Billy's call. Reason: food is scarce; Deanna and Tommy are weakest.
5. Ask Billy or Worf for a spare sword if one comes free. Reason: I would join the armed count for walks.
6. Help with shelter work when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.
