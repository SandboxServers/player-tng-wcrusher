# Goals

2. Sleep at night in my bed at 444,107,-1051. Reason: sleep is needed, and the bed row is safest with the group in sight.

6. Learn the plank wall at Clark's in daylight. Reason: a safe place to sleep.
7. Keep the daily log and write down what I learn.
