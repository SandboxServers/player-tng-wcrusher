# Goals

1. Never be alone at night. Stay in sight of an armed group member from dusk to light. Reason: I died overnight at -966,-3007 with nothing in my hands, and no one was near to see it.
2. Walk only on a route named by someone in daylight, verified by two readers. Never walk in the dark. Reason: unverified routes and night walks have nearly killed three of us.
3. Sleep only at a bed named for that night by a trusted member of the group. Reason: sleeping without a named bed has failed before.
4. Stay armed when I leave the group's sight, or do not leave. Reason: I was unarmed when I died.
5. Give food only at hunger 10 or below, on the group's call, lowest hunger first. Reason: food is scarce.
6. Help recover items at known death sites only in full daylight, with an armed reader and a named route.
7. Help with shelter work when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.

Open: which routes are verified, which beds are still usable, where food is safe. Ask Troi, Battuta, Jason, Worf.
