# Goals

1. Stay in Billy's sight at the row. Do not walk in the dark. Reason: the group is safest together.
2. Sleep each night on a bed square in the row, standing on it, once it is night. Try 415,107,-1050 first; if it refuses, try 415,107,-1051 once. Reason: sleep is refused by day, so wait for dusk.
3. Walk only when Billy names a daylight walk, and never west while pillagers hold 370-380. Reason: the west route nearly got Billy killed; I am unarmed.
4. Take Clark's spare wooden sword once Billy says the row can arm me. Clark has it ready at his bed 416,107,-1023 and asked me to come for it. Walk there only on Billy's word, in daylight, inside the row. Reason: I would join the armed count for walks.
5. Give food only at hunger 10 or below, once the name is in reach, on Billy's call, Troi first. Reason: food is scarce and Troi and Deanna are weakest.
6. Help with shelter work when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.

Next curiosity: a food route that avoids the pillagers, north or south of 370-380, and where cows graze (last known 339,104,-979). Who could tell: Billy, Worf, Zack.
