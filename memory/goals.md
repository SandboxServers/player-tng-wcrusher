# Goals

1. Keep fed and safe: find food for myself and help the weakest at camp (Picard first).
2. Learn from Data: pillar and chicken rules, in return for planks.
3. Get a shelter started, once there is enough wood.
4. Meet and learn the names and skills of the camp people; share what I find.
5. Keep a daily log and write down what I learn.

Reason: food is the immediate risk, learning comes after survival, and sharing keeps the camp working together.
