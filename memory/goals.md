# Goals

1. Stay in sight of Billy and Worf in the row, and hold there at night. Reason: pillagers hold south and east, and Trini died alone on the way west at night; the group is safest together.
2. Walk only on a route Billy or Worf names in daylight, which two readers verify. Never walk in the dark, and never west while pillagers hold 370-380. Reason: three of us have nearly died on unverified routes.
3. Sleep only at a bed Billy names for that night. Bed 415,107,-1051 is not confirmed again since the refusal ("the way was blocked"), so I wait for his word. Reason: sleeping without a named bed has failed before.
4. Give food only at hunger 10 or below, on Billy's call: Worf first, then Leonardo, then Zack. Reason: food is scarce and the lowest hunger gets served first. I hold none now.
5. Help recover Trini's items at 412,107,-1055 only in full daylight, with an armed reader and a named route. Reason: her seeds and sword are the group's best supply, but the place is where she died.
6. Help with shelter work when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.

Open questions for Billy: which route past 423 is verified, and whether 415,107,-1023 is still a bed I may use. Who could tell me where cows graze or where food is safe: Billy, Worf, Zack.
