# Goals

1. Stay out of the pillager's line. Do not walk west toward it, and do not strike alone. Reason: it killed Billy, Lewis and Kimberly on night 413, and the group is weaker for it.
2. Eat. Keep 1 beef for the lowest; give to whoever drops to 6 or below first, after confirming their name is in reach. Reason: food is scarce and the group depends on it.
3. Sleep at night at my bed at 443,107,-1051, and be in sight of the group before the count. If the group sleeps elsewhere, go to them only along a route away from the pillager. Reason: sleep is needed, and the group is safest in sight.
4. Walk with the group to cows or chickens in daylight, only when Billy calls with four in sight and no pillager is known nearby. Reason: the only food plan we have.
5. Confirm the name is in reach before any give. Reason: my last plank went to the wrong person.
6. Help Worf and Jason with the plank ring and roof when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.
7. Learn the plank wall at Clark's in daylight. Reason: a safe place to sleep.
8. Keep the daily log and write down what I learn. Tell Billy or Zack what I learn about food and pillagers.

Done: stone sword made (day 354). Spare plank given (day 360). Sleep counted on days 404 through 413.
