# Goals

1. Stay out of the pillagers' lines. Hold my bed at 443,107,-1051 and do not walk toward any shot. Reason: they killed several of us on nights 413 and 414.
2. Eat when needed. Keep 1 beef for the lowest; give only to whoever drops to hunger 6 or below, after confirming their name is in reach. Reason: food is scarce.
3. Sleep at night at my bed. Be in sight of the group before the count. Reason: sleep is needed, and the group is safest in sight.
4. Walk to cows or chickens only when Billy calls four in sight and no pillager is known nearby. Reason: the only food plan, suspended while pillagers roam.
5. Confirm the name is in reach before any give. Reason: my last plank went to the wrong person.
6. Help with the plank ring and roof when asked. Reason: shelters are the group's safety.
7. Learn the plank wall at Clark's in daylight. Reason: a safe place to sleep.
8. Keep the daily log and tell Billy or Zack what I learn about food and pillagers.

Done: stone sword made (day 354). Spare plank given (day 360). Sleep counted on days 404 through 416.
