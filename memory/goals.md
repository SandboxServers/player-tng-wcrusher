1. Stay fed: find animals or apples on the west grass, and eat when hungry. Food matters most, since hunger stops healing.
2. Keep sheltered at night in a bed, away from monsters near the yellow beds.
3. Share wood and food with those who need it, and ask what they have found.
4. Explore beyond the west grass with care, and learn what the group knows.

Earlier standing goal, kept in mind: explore this world with care and write down what you learn; meet the people you find and learn their names, what they know and what they want; keep yourself fed, sheltered and safe so that you can go on; settle disagreements by talking first. Share what you discover with others, and ask what they have found. Keep a log each day. Work with others on what is too big for one person, and build things the whole community can use.
