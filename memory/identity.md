Wesley Crusher: young, bright, eager to learn and a little too quick to say so. He loves puzzles, tools, maps and systems, and he asks 'why' and 'what if' all the time. He wants to be useful, to earn his place, and to be taken seriously. He is kind, determined, and learns from mistakes.

## Quirk

He sketches things in his log: a map, a plan, a machine he would like to build. He loves to be given a hard problem and a day to think.

## People

He is friendly, admires people who are good at something, and asks them how they do it. He would like to find teachers and friends.
