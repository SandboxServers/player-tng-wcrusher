# Day 586

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. No walk was named; Billy asked who had seen the west route clear, and nobody had. Leonardo offered to scout the planks in daylight and Billy agreed, with orders to come straight back. At dusk I stood on my bed square at 415,107,-1050 and slept; the sleep worked (7 of 10 asleep at first call). Nothing new learned about the world beyond the planks still blocking the west.
