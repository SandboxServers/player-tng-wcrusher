# Day 356

Quiet day at the bed row at 430,107,-1037. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. The group held for Billy's cow walk and no call came. Lewis, Trini and Kimberly were the lowest on food (14-17 hunger), with Leonardo and Picard holding chicken and melon for them. An enderman was seen near Lewis, who walked back to the row. Night came and I slept in the white bed with the group in reach. Nothing new was learned beyond the seal of oak planks in a ring with a roof, which Kimberly passed on from Worf and Riker.
