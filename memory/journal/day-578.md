# Day 578

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. No walk was named, so none was taken. Billy gave potatoes to Zack and Tommy; I kept my five for anyone at hunger 6 or below, and nobody in reach was that low. Deanna is at health 8, the weakest. Jason and Worf were sharing the bed row and some beds were blocked, so Billy re-named beds several times. At dusk I stayed at my bed 415,107,-1050, in sight, and tried to sleep at night.
