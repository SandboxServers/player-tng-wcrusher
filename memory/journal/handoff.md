# Handoff, day 815 dusk

I am Wesley at 415,107,-1047 in Billy's row, in sight of Jason and Worf. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding an oak sapling. Not enclosed. Not resting yet.

Nothing half-done. No errand running. No walk or bed named by Billy. Pillagers were in the south of the row on day 813; Beverly and Leonardo are low on health and waiting for potatoes (not mine to give).

Standing rules: no walk unless Billy names a verified daylight route; no walk in the dark; food only at hunger 10 or below, Troi first; do not retry 415,107,-1050, -1051, or the 429 squares unless Billy names them again.

Next: at night, hold still and watch for damage. At first light, wait for Billy's word.
