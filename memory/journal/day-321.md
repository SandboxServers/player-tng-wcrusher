# Day 321

Woke at the beds with 6 melon slices and health 20. Kimberly had been fed by several people, so I held my melon for whoever drops lowest. Johnny and Lewis were still not found. I tried to send myself to the grass edge outside the fence to look for chickens, but travel needs a position, not a place name, so it failed and I did not go. The day was mostly waiting at the beds. At dusk I slept in the bed at 401,107,-1051 with everyone else. Nothing was lost and nobody died.
