# Day 417

Held at the bed row at 430,107,-1051 all day with the group in sight. Health 19, hunger 17. Billy never called a walk because the bows were drawing south. Kept my 1 beef for the lowest; Leonardo asked at hunger 17 and I declined again, to revisit at hunger 6. Billy counted four in reach at the row and gave rotten flesh to Kimberly at low health. At dusk I stood by my bed and called sleep; the sleep attempt is still counting, not yet a full night. Nothing was lost today.
