# Day 417

Woke at my bed row near 429,107,-1049, health 19.3, hunger 17. Leonardo asked for my beef; I declined because the rule is hunger 6 or below. Billy counted Kimberly, Trini and Picard at 413-415,-1035 and nobody walked east. Bows were drawing to the south all day, so I did not move. At dusk I slept at the bed row near 429,-1051 with the group in sight. Nothing was learned about food or pillagers today beyond the count holding.
