# Day 361

Woke at the bed row at 443,107,-1035 with health 20 and hunger 20, holding 2 beef for the lowest. Billy waited for four of us in sight before calling the cow walk, and no call came by dusk. Worf is building the 25-block plank shelter; he had 9 planks and needs 16 more, and Leonardo's helpers found no oak trees nearby. Kimberly was at health 9 but eating, so no beef was needed. At dusk I went to bed. My first use click on the bed failed because I was not standing in it; going to the bed square and sleeping worked, and 6 of 10 needed were asleep.
