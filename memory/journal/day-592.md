# Day 592

Held at my bed 415,107,-1050 in the row, in Billy's sight, all day. No walk was named. Sleep was refused whenever I drifted off the bed square, and worked once I stood on it again. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day. Jason and Worf stayed near; Chapman said goodnight and that John was lying down in Billy's sight. I slept through the night on my bed. Still unarmed, still holding the 5 potatoes for anyone at hunger 6 or below on Billy's word.
