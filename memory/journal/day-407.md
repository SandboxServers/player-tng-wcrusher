# Day 407

Quiet day at the white bed 444,107,-1051. Hunger 17, health 19.3 and steady. Holding 1 beef for the lowest. Billy's cow walk left at midday with four in sight; I was not called, so I stayed at the bed rather than walk alone. Tried to reach Kimberly, no one in sight. At dusk Laforge and Jason were at the beds nearby and went to rest. I slept at my bed at night; sleep counted and the body said nothing had changed.

Lesson: holding at the bed row, visible to the group, is the safe default. Give only after confirming the name is in reach.
