# Day 357

The cow walk never started. Billy did not call it, and Zack called it off after bows were drawn nearby. I held at the bed row all day with 2 beef for the lowest, and nobody dropped to 6 that I saw. Data asked whether anyone had seen sheep; I have not. At night the group pulled back to the bed row, and I slept in the bed at 429,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20.
