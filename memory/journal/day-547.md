# Day 547

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, hunger 18, health 20. The cow walk started at dawn with Billy, Jason and Lewis, but pillagers with crossbows hold the way west near 370-380, -1022 to -1029, so Billy turned back and no walk was named after. I did not walk. Billy's cobblestone and rotten flesh lie at 374,99,-1029 by the pillagers. Food is still the group's problem; Riker and Lewis are the weakest. I slept at my bed 415,107,-1050 at night (8 of 10 asleep). Lesson: holding in sight kept me safe, and walks only go when the way is clear.
