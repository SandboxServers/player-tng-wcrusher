# Day 412

Day 412 was spent at the bed row with Billy's group. I counted in at first light, but the cow walk was called off and then postponed, so we held at the row all day. I tried to follow Billy out to the cows at the morning call, but the follow failed because he was out of my sight, so I walked back to the row and stayed in sight. Health stayed at 19.3 and hunger at 17 all day, so no food was needed yet. Riker (health 8.4) and Leonardo are the ones to watch. I am holding 1 beef for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below, after confirming the name is in reach. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept; sleep is counted 3 of 10 for this stretch. Lesson: a follow fails once the leader is out of sight, so stay in sight first.
