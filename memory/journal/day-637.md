# Day 637

Held in the row all day with Billy's sight, no walk named. Slept at bed 415,107,-1050 on the night of 635 and again at dusk on 637. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nobody has seen sheep grazing; Leonardo keeps asking. MacDonald (health 7) and Data (health 13) are the weakest; food is to go to Troi first, then MacDonald, on Billy's word only.
