# Day 637

Held in Billy's sight at the row all day with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, holding an oak sapling. Heard the row's voices: Kimberly was hit twice from behind near 413,107,-1041, Troi and Deanna are at 14-16 health, MacDonald at 7. At dusk I moved onto the bed square 415,107,-1050 and slept; the sleep was accepted with 6 of 10 asleep. Nothing new was learned; the day was for holding and listening.
