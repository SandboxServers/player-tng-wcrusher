# Day 322

Woke at the beds at 402,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, 6 melon slices still held for the weakest. Tommy went to the chicken field north-east to bring chickens back; I asked him where it is but no reply came. Leonardo and Johnny were fed by Jason and Picard with chicken and melon. Macdonald walked me and Chapman to the cow spot at 368,94,-1118 in daylight with his sword; no cows were in sight there. I have no sword, so I only walk along and carry. Slept at dusk in the bed at 401,107,-1051. Nobody died. Still no chicken position and no food found myself.
