# Day 322

Woke at the beds with 6 melon, health and hunger full. Walked with Macdonald to the cow spot 368,94,-1118; no cows there. Chicken field still not found; Tommy went to the north-east field. Leonardo and Picard looked weak; Johnny and Leonardo were reported fed. Went to bed at the beds 401,107,-1050 with others nearby. Nobody died. Next: ask Tommy for the chicken field position, join the next cow trip, keep melon for the weakest.
