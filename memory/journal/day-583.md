# Day 583

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight all day. Billy named no walk: Tommy and Worf both report solid oak planks blocking the west path, and Billy will only name a route once one is verified. I was not sent anywhere, and no food was needed (health 20, hunger 20). Many people spoke at dusk and dawn; most were holding and waiting. I slept at night at my bed. Nothing new learned except that the group is patient and that the west stays closed for now.
