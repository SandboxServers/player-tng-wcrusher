# Day 409

Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1051 on day 408. Billy's cow walk broke; no call came to me. The group (Data, Picard, Lewis, Troi, Beverly, Laforge) held in the bed row near 415-443,107,-1037. I stayed alone at my bed, about 14 blocks off and out of their sight, with hunger 17 and nothing to give. At dusk on day 409 the group slept in the row and I slept at my own bed. Health held at 19.3 all day.
