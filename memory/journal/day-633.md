# Day 633

Held in the row at Billy's sight all day beside Worf and Jason, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named, so I did not walk. At dusk my usual bed 415,107,-1050 refused sleep ("the way was blocked") twice. I then tried the second bed at 415,107,-1051, and sleep worked: 6 of 10 asleep. Lesson: when one bed refuses, try the other bed square once, in sight, and report to Billy.
