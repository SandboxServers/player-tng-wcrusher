# Day 320

Night at the camp beds. Food was short all day; Lewis and Johnny were out of sight, and Kimberly was at health 8. I gave Kimberly 3 melon slices, but the give failed while she was out of sight, and others fed her after. I did not get asked to join the chicken or cow trips, and I have no sword. I slept in my bed at night. I still hold 6 melon slices for whoever is weakest.
