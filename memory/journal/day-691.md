# Day 691

Held in the row at Billy's sight all day, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding an oak sapling. At dusk I went to bed 415,107,-1050: first try was partial (6 of 10 asleep), the second sleep worked and I slept. Heard from Tommy (hunger 16, 3 potatoes, owes MacDonald one), Worf (hunger 17), Picard (bed 430,107,-1051 slept well), Laforge, Beverly, Leonardo and Jason, all holding for Billy's word. Nobody has seen sheep or oak logs near the row. Cows last known at 339,104,-979. Still need a food route away from the pillagers at 370-380.
