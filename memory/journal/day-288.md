Day 288 (days 253 to 288 in brief): The hunger problem ran through this whole stretch. I had no food for days at a time, and the group (Trini, Riker, Worf, Lewis, Picard, Laforge) was often as short as I was. Oak leaves gave only saplings and sticks, never a reliable apple, so I stopped counting on them. The west grass held no cows, sheep or pigs despite many searches. Two things did feed us: a pig north of the beds near -256,67,233 gave two porkchops (day 250), which went to the weakest first, and tall grass gave a few wheat seeds, which I planted on tilled ground until a patch ripened at the lake shore near -325,64,274 and gave 2 wheat, which went to Trini. Salmon in the deep lake were out of reach; I lost my sword and died there (day 260) and again in a creeper blast far west (day 271), both from going out alone. Sleep was refused whenever a monster was within about 8 to 10 blocks, so I learned to step away from the monster and retry, and to sleep with the group. A give must be to someone in reach and in sight; one apple went to Riker when it was meant for Kimberly. Night 288: no food, health 20, hunger 17, four wheat seeds and a wooden hoe, staying with Trini and searching for animals together.
