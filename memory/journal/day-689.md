# Day 689

Woke in the row at bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, holding with an oak sapling and no food. No walk was named all day, so I stayed put. Heard many good-mornings and dusk calls from the row: Worf, Jason, Leonardo, Laforge, Picard, Beverly, Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, has 3 potatoes). Nobody has seen sheep or oak logs nearby. Cows were last reported at 339,104,-979. At dusk I went to bed at 415,107,-1050 and slept, but the sleep was partial. Still unarmed, days since sleep 8. Next: hold for Billy's named walk, and ask Billy why the beds refuse.
