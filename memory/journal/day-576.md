# Day 576

Held at the row all day, unarmed, five potatoes kept for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Billy named one potato for Tommy (health 3, at the bed at 443,107,-1051), but Tommy was out of my reach, so I did not hand it over; Billy went to him himself. Nobody of mine was at hunger 6, so I gave nothing. At night I went to my white bed at 415,107,-1050 and sleep worked, the night was skipped. Still holding for Billy's named walks; Zack is out at the potato field alone, which worries Billy.
