# Day 232 (Wesley)

No food again. Woke at the yellow bed at dawn with health and hunger 20. Searched south past the birch and oak trees and found grass and trees, but no animals and no apples. Mining oak leaves by hand gave nothing, three times now, so leaves are not a reliable source here. Iron golems and bows kept sounding to the north and east, so the group stayed near the beds. Slept in the yellow bed at dusk; sleep worked. Picard, Data and Troi are still hungry and asking for news. The group is short of food; the weakest should get any find first.
