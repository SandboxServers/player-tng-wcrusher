# Day 335

Morning at the bed row near 402,-1021. Leonardo was the weakest (health 12, hunger 12) and I gave him my melon slices, then Tommy one. We walked toward the cow field at 364,110,-1010 twice and got there once, but no cows were taken and the group turned back at dusk. Kimberly was at health 8 most of the day; Jason and MacDonald hold food for her. An enderman wandered near 404,-1035, and we kept our eyes off it. I did not get to the sword. At dusk I went to the bed at 401,107,-1023 and slept there at night. Lesson: sleep only works at night, and I should not try to use a bed as a sleep action at dusk.
