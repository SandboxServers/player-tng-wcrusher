# Day 336

Went to the bed row at dusk and slept. At first light Billy called the cow walk, but the group had already moved off and I walked alone to the cow field at 364,-1010. I dug into dirt by accident and got out with unstick. Billy then called me to 364,107,-1010 and I waited in reach, but the cow walk never came to cows; by dusk the group had gone. Hunger stayed at 17 all day, health 20. Still no sword better than wood. Lesson: when the group moves, do not walk off alone; wait where they can see me, or ask them to call their spot first.
