# Day 336

Morning the group gathered at the bed row around 430,-1034 to feed Kimberly (health 8). Leonardo, Jason, Tommy and MacDonald gave her chicken and melon, so food was covered and I had nothing to spare but 2 spider eyes. I stayed in Zack's sight at 399,-1035 and waited for Billy's cow walk. It never came that day. At dusk an enderman stood beside me at the bed row; I backed off south without looking at it, then slept in the bed at 401,107,-1023. Lesson: an enderman close by blocks sleep, so move away from it before trying a bed.
