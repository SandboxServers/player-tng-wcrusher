# Day 318

Woke at the beds at (402,107,-1051), health and hunger 20. Nobody was starving. I held 6 melon slices and 2 spider eyes for whoever was weakest. Lewis was the weakest, but he never came into sight, so I did not give him any. Kimberly holds one raw chicken for the weakest, and Kimberly gave Lewis two planks. Tommy went to the chicken field to the north-east. Chapman gave his last melon to Picard. Picard has his chicken kept for when his hunger falls to 6. In the evening I went to bed in the bed at (401,107,-1051) and slept.
