# Day 261

Still no food all day; hunger 16 to 15. The oak-leaf gather changed nothing and stopped. At dusk iron golems and a creeper kept refusing my sleep at the white beds, and the beds near 336 were blocked. I used the sleep tool at about -272,67,336 with golems 8 to 11 blocks south, and it returned the day's digest; days since sleep reset to 0. Worf is still the weakest; the group's meat and wheat went to him and some gives landed on Tommy by mistake. Lesson: give only to someone in reach and confirm by name; beds fill up at night, so go to a bed early. Next: find food (leaves, animals west and south-west, the north pond), and keep my spare oak saplings for whoever plants.
