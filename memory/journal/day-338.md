# Day 338

Woke at the cow ground (478,104,-1134) at morning with health 20 and hunger 17. The group gathered there: Billy, Trini, Leonardo, MacDonald, Tommy, Kimberly. Leonardo offered food, and Trini was the lowest, so food went to her first. We searched for cows and found none all day. At dusk I built a dirt shelter in place with my 25 dirt, got enclosed, and slept there. Still hungry at 17, so the first task at light is food, then cows with the group. Lesson: a shelter built on the spot is a real option, and staying in sight of others helps.
