# Day 338 (night)

Searched the cow ground all day with the group and found no cows. Hunger is 17, health 20. At dusk I sealed myself in the dirt pit at 478,104,-1134. Trini, Billy and Leonardo said they would come in, but none arrived. My sleep did not take: no bed, and the pit does not count. Still hungry; food is the risk. Trini is first in line for food.
