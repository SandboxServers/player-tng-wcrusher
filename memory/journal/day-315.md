# Day 315

Woke at midday with full health and hunger. I had traded my beef to MacDonald for 5 oak planks, and he later handed them over. Food is scarce at the camp: no cows, pigs or beef nearby, only chickens to the north and south-east. Picard is at health 2 and is the weakest; Worf died in the night and needs food. I have no food left, only rotten flesh and a spider eye. Data offered to share what he has learned about pillars and the chicken rule in exchange for planks; I intend to take that trade in the morning. I slept in a white bed near the chest after dusk. The first bed attempts failed until night came; the sleep call worked.
