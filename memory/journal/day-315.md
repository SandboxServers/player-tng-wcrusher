# Day 315

I woke at the camp beds with nothing but 2 rotten flesh, a spider eye and 5 oak planks. I went east to the cow I had seen; it was gone, and no animals were there. I walked back to the white beds, offered the rotten flesh to Picard (weakest, health 2) and to Zack, who offered feathers and wool for it. Picard is first in line for any meat. Data wants planks for a shelter, and Riker holds 9 for him. I slept at the white bed near the chest. Food is still the main risk; no cows or pigs are known near camp.
