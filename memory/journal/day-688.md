# Day 688

Held in the row at Billy's group all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no walk named. Heard from Worf of cows at 434,106,-1113 besides 339,104,-979; no sheep seen by anyone. Beds 415,107,-1050 and -1051 refused sleep twice on the night of 687 ("the way was blocked"), then at dusk 688 bed -1050 worked and I slept, 2 of 10 asleep. Asked Billy in speech why the beds refuse; no answer yet.
