# Day 330

Mattered today: food and the search for Johnny. I gave one melon to Picard when he was hungry in reach. I offered my wooden sword and 4 melon to Jason, Tommy and Zack for the cow search, but I never got into their sight, so I did not walk out with them. I learned again that sleep is refused until true night, and that gives and follows only work when the person is in sight. Next: join the next cow walk in sight, ask Macdonald where the cows graze, and find the chicken field. Tell Chapman and Trini what I learned about the cow positions (364,-1010 and 370,93,-1124).
