# Day 545

Dawn to dusk at the row beside my bed, unarmed, health 20, hunger 18, no food. Billy kept his group holding in his sight all day and named no walk, because the armed count stayed at four or fewer (Picard, Jason, MacDonald, Clark, Worf, Riker). I held, did not walk, and gave nothing. At night I slept at my bed beside the row (9 of 10 counted asleep, day 546 dawn wake). Nothing new was learned; the group is short of food, and Lewis is at health 7.
