# Day 290 (Wesley)

Food was still the whole problem. I gave my three wheat seeds to Jason for the tilled patch by the north beds, then spent the day searching west and south-east with Trini and Billy and found no animals. Hunger fell from 17 to 16 with no food on me. Sleep was refused several times near the beds while mobs were about, and I finally slept at the north white bed at night 290. Zack and Leonardo (both hunger 10) were the lowest; Troi held LaForge's chicken for them and it went through Troi's hands to Leonardo. Lesson: stay with the group, give to the lowest in sight, and keep trying sleep at a bed only after the mobs move off.
