# Day 358

Quiet day at the bed row at 429,107,-1037. No cow call from Billy. Good morning said. Data waited for Worf's white wool, and Worf came to him. Bows were drawn to the southwest, so the group pulled back to the bed row. Picard, Leonardo and Worf all held food for the lowest. I held 2 beef for the lowest and did not need to give it. I slept in the white bed at night with the group in reach. Health 20, hunger 20.
