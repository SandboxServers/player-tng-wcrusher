# Day 582

Held at my white bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy named no walk: Tommy reported the path west blocked by oak planks, so everyone held. Tommy has two spare wooden swords and promised one to Beverly once she is beside him in the row on Billy's word. Nobody was at hunger 6 or below, so my five potatoes stayed in my pack. At night I stood at my bed and slept; Billy said no walk until morning. Nothing was hurt.
