# Day 404

Held at the bed row 444,107,-1051 all day with 1 beef for the lowest, health 19, hunger 17. Billy's walk call was heard, but my follow failed because I could not see him, so I held at the row as did others. Tommy fell to health 2 at night and was fed by Kimberly, Trini and Jason; Trini then died at 412,107,-1040 and her things lie there. Sleep at dusk said 3 of 10 asleep, so the count likely did not reset. Lesson: follow only works when the leader is in sight; wait at the row for a call into view.
