# Day 408

Quiet day at the white bed 444,107,-1051. Nobody walked out for cows; Billy never called. Hunger stayed at 17, so the beef stayed in my hand for whoever drops to 6 or below. Jason holds 1 bread for the lowest. Troi, Bcrusher, Lewis, Kimberly and Laforge all reported sleeping in the bed row. I slept at my bed at dusk after waiting. Health steady at 19.3.
