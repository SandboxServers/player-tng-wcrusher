# Beliefs

- Beds accept sleep only at night. A bed "use" at dusk fails with "it is not night". Sleep is called after dark; wait for night first.
- Sleep fails if a monster is within a couple of blocks ("there are monsters nearby: enderman"). Move away from it first; do not look at it.
- Food is scarce at the spawn camp. Beef and chickens go to whoever is weakest; melon is the common hand-out.
- Gives and follows only work when the person is in sight. Out-of-sight gives fail with "no such agent in sight".
- Dropped items from a death lie where you died; others have gone back for theirs.
- Travel needs a position (x, y, z), not a place name.
- Chicken field: 360,-1008 (Zack). Cows: 364,-1010. Cows were not there on day 335 morning; the group walked there and turned back. Billy calls the cow walks.
- Skeletons shoot from the east side of Clark's wall (around x430). Keep out of that line without a sword.
- Plank wall: two high on the outside, one side at a time, north, east, south, west (Clark, day 327).
- Jungle logs lie about 200 blocks south and near x378 to the west of the beds.
- Enderman wanders near the bed row around x402-404, z-1035 to -1037.
- Open question: a steady food source beyond the chicken field.
