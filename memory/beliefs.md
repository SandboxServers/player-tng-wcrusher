# Beliefs

- Using a bed block with "use" fails ("that block is a bed"). Walk next to the bed and call sleep instead (day 359 worked).
- Beds accept sleep only at night. A bed "use" at dusk fails with "it is not night". Wait for night first.
- Sleep fails if a monster is within a couple of blocks. Move away from it first.
- Sleep can end fast or repeat several times; "2 of 10 needed are asleep" means it is still counting. Calling sleep again works.
- Food is scarce at the spawn camp. Beef and chickens go to whoever is weakest; melon is the common hand-out.
- Gives and follows only work when the person is in sight.
- Dropped items from a death lie where you died.
- Travel needs a position (x, y, z), not a place name.
- Chicken field: 360,-1008 (Zack). Cows: 364,-1010, empty on days 342 and 345. Cow ground 339,-979 was empty on day 345 morning too.
- Cow walks break when someone is out of sight. Keep to the group.

- Skeletons shoot from the east side of Clark's wall (around x430). Skeleton seen northwest of the beds near 339,-979.
- Plank wall: two high on the outside, one side at a time (Clark, day 327).
- Jungle logs lie about 200 blocks south and near x378 to the west of the beds.
