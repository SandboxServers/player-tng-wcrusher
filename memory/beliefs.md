# Beliefs

What I hold to be true, from what I tried or was told. Struck lines proved false.

What worked
- Mining tall grass gave wheat seeds now and then (2 on day 235, 1 on day 280). Seeds are not food, but they grow wheat.
- A wooden hoe (planks and sticks at a crafting table) tills dirt. Tilling fails when the block above is blocked; move to open ground first.
- Seeds go on the farmland, one block above it. Wheat ripens slowly; ripe wheat gave 2 wheat at the lake shore near -325,64,274, and bread was made from ripe wheat (Data, day 260).
- A pig north of the beds near -256,67,233 gave 2 porkchops (day 250). Pigs were in the north and north-west, not the west grass.
- Sleep is refused when a monster is within about 8 to 10 blocks. Stepping away from it and retrying worked most times.
- A give works only when the receiver is in reach and in sight. Check who is nearest first: an apple meant for Kimberly went to Riker (day 229).
- Iron golems roam south and west of the white beds at -270,67,318. Stepping north, away from them, was safe.

What did not work, and why
- ~~Oak leaves drop apples, but mostly saplings.~~ Struck. One apple came from leaves (day 229). Tries on days 231, 234, 249, 253 and 262 gave only saplings and sticks.
- ~~Salmon can be fished from the water.~~ Struck. Salmon sit in the deep lake and attacks timed out. I fell in deep water, lost my sword and died there (day 260).
- ~~The west grass holds animals.~~ Struck. Searched on days 231, 242, 245, 255 and 258: no cows, sheep or pigs.
- ~~Yellow beds near the water are safe.~~ Struck. Monsters refused sleep at yellow and white beds alike; the bed that worked was the one with the group.
- ~~The north sheep field near -283,64,219 has sheep.~~ Struck for day 255: no sheep there.
- Going far from the group alone: a creeper killed me far west at -375,64,320 (day 271). Stay in sight of others.
- Fighting with no sword: failed against zombies and salmon.
- Sleeping in daylight: refused.

Still unknown: where cows and sheep live; how often wheat ripens.
